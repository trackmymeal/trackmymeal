# TrackMyMeal

**Food tracking. On your terms.**

TrackMyMeal is an upcoming **free, open-source food and calorie tracking application** designed to make tracking nutrition simpler, more accessible, and entirely under your control.

🌐 **Website:** https://trackmymeal.io

## What we're building

Our vision is a modern alternative to traditional calorie counters and nutrition tracking apps, with a focus on simplicity, transparency, and data ownership.

Planned features include:

- **Calorie & Macro Tracking** — Track calories, protein, carbohydrates, fats, and daily nutrition goals.
- **Food Database** — Search foods, scan barcodes, and create custom food entries.
- **Reusable Meals** — Save your favorite meals and quickly log them again.
- **AI & MCP Integration** — Connect compatible AI assistants through the Model Context Protocol to manage food logs with user-authorized access.
- **Your Data, Your Control** — Export your nutrition history and manage private or shared food entries.
- **Free & Open Source** — Built around accessibility, transparency, and freedom from ads and paywalls.

## Project status

🚧 **In development**

TrackMyMeal is in its early planning stages. The features described above represent our current vision and may evolve as development progresses.

The application source code will be published as development progresses.

Learn more at **https://trackmymeal.io**.
